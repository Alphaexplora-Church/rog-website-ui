## Proposed direction

Build this as the H.I.M. Philippines page inside the current River of God site. Follow the shared design standards in `DESIGN.md` and reuse the existing ROG navigation, buttons, typography, plate colors, and footer. Keep the official HIM mark in its red-orange brand colors and use the existing source portraits without changing who is shown.

The implemented page uses the ROG floating glass navigation pill; a full-bleed, darkened congregation photograph; black, graphite, and light plates; compact portrait directory rows; numbered disclosures; and cyan only for small focus and state cues. It removes the crowded old navigation bar, large orange panels, gold map illustration, and mountain-backed application strip from the earlier concept. Do not introduce broad saturated color fills.

The page should make the full story, mission, vision, all 16 profile placements, all 12 core values, all 8 faith statements, application path, contact information, and source-page footer details easy to scan. Preserve the full source wording in implementation. Do not shorten, silently correct, or replace it with paraphrased body copy.
